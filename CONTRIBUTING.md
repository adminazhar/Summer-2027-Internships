# Contributing

The tables are rebuilt every 4 hours from [TalentdPro](https://www.talentdpro.com/us/entry-level-jobs?utm_source=github&utm_medium=contributing&utm_campaign=us-internships)'s job database, so edits to the Markdown files would be overwritten. To change the list, open an issue instead:

- **[Add a role](../../issues/new?template=new_role.yml)**: an open internship or new grad role at a US company. Please link the employer's own posting rather than a job board.
- **[Report a closed role or a mistake](../../issues/new?template=fix_role.yml)**: closed postings, broken links, wrong location or pay.

Each issue is checked by hand. Accepted roles appear in the next refresh, and the issue is closed with a note.
